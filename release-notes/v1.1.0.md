# Prepare to Co-op Edition v1.1.0

**A major update over 1.0.0.** The session is now one shared world with teamwork rules: boss fights and invasions are won or lost together, a death no longer brings the world back for anyone, and a long list of fixes keeps every game in a session in step, from bosses and NPCs to key items and invasions.

**Includes the [DS1 PTDE FPS Unlock](https://github.com/zofrea1/DS1-FPS-Unlock-PTDE-Remastered) 1.8.0**, built into the same `xinput1_3.dll`. New there: feet and hips no longer bob on stairs at a high frame rate, the stamina bar no longer keeps a yellow tail after a sprint, and slides keep the ground at any frame rate.

## What's new

- **Spectate, then win or wipe together.** Fall during a boss fight (yours, or a teammate's anywhere in the world) or an invasion and the camera follows a teammate who is still standing, drawn in full detail even far away (switch with the D-pad or the arrow keys). If the team wins, the fallen respawn and the boss stays dead. If everyone falls, all games load together into a world that starts over, as after a single-player death.
- **A death keeps the world.** Fall in a session and you respawn at your bonfire with the enemies the team has killed still dead, host and guests alike, so everyone keeps playing in the same world. Enemies come back at a bonfire rest, or for everyone after a team wipe. (Before, a fallen player's game brought every enemy back for that player alone.)
- **Crimson Blossoms are on by default** (`DeathDebuffs`). Each death in a session costs 10% of your maximum HP, up to five times, until your next bonfire rest.
- **Rests and boss fights.** Nobody can rest while a standing teammate fights a boss. A rest always brings a spectator back; after a lost fight it reloads everyone in the area with them, so the boss starts over for everyone. The player who rested is told when the others wait for them to leave the bonfire.
- **Bosses behave.** After a wipe, bosses start in their place, wake on cue and fight whoever is in the room. A standing player's game moves them, never a spectator's, so a boss no longer wanders back to a fallen player's body or freezes on its perch.
- **Invasions, done properly.** Invaders arrive at the game's own invasion points (now in the Asylum and Firelink too) and see the host's and guests' health bars; multiplayer walls come down for them. While an invader is in the world, boss fog stays shut, and while a boss fight is on, no invader can find the world. The invader wins only when every co-op player is down, and every invasion has exactly one outcome.
- **Invasions pay** (`InvasionRewards`). An invader who defeats every co-op player earns a Humanity for each of them; if the invader falls, the host and every guest earn one, spectators included.
- **Progress comes home.** Key items picked up in another world always reach your own save. Oscar no longer dies in a guest's game while the host's game loads, and his fate carries home with you. A host's World Tendency disasters no longer follow a guest home.
- **Health bars match the scaling**, another player's Skull Lantern, Cast Light or Sunlight Maggot lights your game too, and notices and health bars show character names instead of Steam names.
- **Cursed Pendant.** Curse your own world (pure black World Tendency), alone or as host, until you use it again.
- **Finding a world.** If a world can't take you (full, busy, mid-boss, gone), the next one found is tried. A host on the title screen is hidden, and Dried Fingers makes your world 50% more likely to be picked by invaders.
- **Safety nets.** A death that never reaches its respawn finishes by itself. Guests can't join a host who is dead or mid-boss.

## Upgrading

Replace `xinput1_3.dll` and `PTDE-FPS-Unlock.ini`, then set your `Password` and FPS Unlock settings again. An old INI also works, but keeps Crimson Blossoms off (`DeathDebuffs = false`). Players on 1.0.0 and 1.1.0 can still find and join each other, but the team rules (spectating, wipes, the rest lock, deaths that keep the world) need everyone on 1.1.0, so update together.

New install: see the README (copy the two files next to `DARKSOULS.exe`, set a shared `Password`, rest at a bonfire for the co-op items). The co-op uses its own save, `DRAKS0005.co2`. Back up your saves.

**Online play: no guarantees.** The mod changes the game in memory; use at your own risk. No responsibility is accepted for bans or lost progress (see the README and LICENSE).

Send `PTDE-FPS-Unlock.log` from every player in the session, and any `*-crash.dmp`, with a bug report.
