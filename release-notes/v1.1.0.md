# Prepare to Co-op Edition v1.1.0

The team update. Boss fights and invasions are now won or lost together: fall, and you watch your friends finish the fight. A wipe reloads every game at the same moment into a world that starts over, just like single player. Underneath, a long list of fixes keeps every game in a session in step: bosses, NPCs, key items and invasions now behave the way the game intends.

**Includes the [DS1 PTDE FPS Unlock](https://github.com/zofrea1/DS1-FPS-Unlock-PTDE-Remastered) 1.8.0**, built into the same `xinput1_3.dll`. New there: feet and hips no longer bob on stairs at a high frame rate, the stamina bar no longer keeps a yellow tail after a sprint, and the step-down fix holds at any frame rate.

## What's new

- **Spectate, then win or wipe together.** Fall during a boss fight or an invasion and the camera follows a teammate who is still standing (switch with the D-pad or the arrow keys). If the team wins, the fallen respawn and the boss stays dead. If everyone falls, all games load together and the world resets as it does after a single-player death. A rest at a bonfire always brings a spectator back; after a lost fight it reloads everyone in the area with them, so the boss starts over for everyone.
- **Bosses behave after a wipe.** After a wipe, bosses start in their place, wake on cue and fight whoever is in the room, whichever player started the fight. (Before, the Asylum Demon could start on the floor, fall through it, or stand idle.)
- **No resting mid-fight.** Nobody can rest while a teammate who is still standing fights a boss, so a rest never resets a fight in progress.
- **Progress comes home.** Key items picked up in another world now always reach your own save. Oscar no longer dies in a guest's game while the host's game loads, and his fate carries home with you. A host's World Tendency disasters no longer follow a guest home.
- **Invasions, done properly.** Invaders arrive at the game's own invasion points (now in the Asylum and Firelink too) and see the host's and guests' health bars. Multiplayer walls come down for them. While an invader is in the world, boss fog stays shut, and while a boss fight is on, no invader can find the world. The invader wins only when every co-op player is down, and every invasion has exactly one outcome.
- **Invasions pay.** An invader who defeats every co-op player earns a Humanity for each of them. If the invader falls, the host and every guest earn one, spectators included (`InvasionRewards`).
- **Health bars match the scaling.** Each game scales its own copy of an enemy, so health bars drain correctly with extra players.
- **Shared light.** Another player's Skull Lantern, Cast Light or Sunlight Maggot lights up your game too.
- **Cursed Pendant.** Curse your own world (pure black World Tendency), alone or as host, until you use it again.
- **Names you know.** Notices and floating health bars show character names, not Steam names.
- **Finding a world.** If a world can't take you (full, busy, mid-boss, gone), the next one found is tried. A host on the title screen is hidden, and Dried Fingers makes your world 50% more likely to be picked by invaders.
- **Safety nets.** A death that never reaches its respawn finishes by itself. Guests can't join a host who is dead or mid-boss.

## Upgrading

Replace `xinput1_3.dll` and `PTDE-FPS-Unlock.ini`, then set your `Password` and FPS Unlock settings again (an old INI also works). Players on 1.0.0 and 1.1.0 can still find and join each other, but the team rules (spectating, wipes, the rest lock) need everyone on 1.1.0, so update together.

New install: see the README (copy the two files next to `DARKSOULS.exe`, set a shared `Password`, rest at a bonfire for the co-op items). The co-op uses its own save, `DRAKS0005.co2`. Back up your saves.

**Online play: no guarantees.** The mod changes the game in memory; use at your own risk. No responsibility is accepted for bans or lost progress (see the README and LICENSE).

Send `PTDE-FPS-Unlock.log` from every player in the session, and any `*-crash.dmp`, with a bug report.
