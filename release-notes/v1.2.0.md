# Prepare to Co-op Edition v1.2.0

Anyone can lead the party to a bonfire, your keys come with you into the host's world, and the other players' characters and warps are fixed in several ways. 1.2.0 plays with 1.0.0 and 1.1.0 (same protocol).

**Includes the [DS1 PTDE FPS Unlock](https://github.com/zofrea1/DS1-FPS-Unlock-PTDE-Remastered) 1.8.0**, as 1.1.0 did.

## What's new

- **Anyone can travel.** A guest's bonfire warp now takes everyone along, to any bonfire the host's own warp could reach (the host owns the Lordvessel and has rested there). A player with a menu open or sitting at a bonfire follows once done. Nobody travels during a boss fight or an invasion, while a teammate is down, or while the world starts over after a lost fight.
- **Your keys come with you.** In the host's world you carry the keys you own that the host has too, so a door the host's world can open opens for you as well.
- **Keep your seat.** A player already sitting at a bonfire when a teammate's boss fight starts stays seated and can still level up, attune, use the Bottomless Box, kindle or reverse hollowing. Only the warp waits.
- **The Darksign** works in a session like the Homeward Bone: the host and every guest go to the host's last bonfire, at the cost of the host's souls and humanity. Neither counts as a rest.
- **YOUR TEAM DIED.** A team wipe says so, in the game's own lettering (English game text). Every other death still says YOU DIED.
- **Invaders can drink Estus**, up to 5 charges per invasion. Any extra charges come back once they're home.

## Fixes

- A guest following a warp, a Homeward Bone or a cutscene now lands at the destination instead of falling through the world.
- After a respawn, a team wipe or a trip home, another player's character could come back with no collision and none of their attacks showing. It's now created only once that player has got up at their bonfire.
- Other players' characters keep updating however far away they are, and an animation stuck on one of them is stopped.
- A player who dies in their own world while joining has the join called off instead of being left half-joined.
- A Homeward Bone and a guest's travel at the same moment no longer warp the host twice.

## Upgrading

Replace `xinput1_3.dll`. There are no new settings, so your 1.1.0 `PTDE-FPS-Unlock.ini` works as it is (coming from 1.0.0, see the [1.1.0 notes](https://github.com/zofrea1/DS1-Prepare-to-Coop-PTDE/blob/main/release-notes/v1.1.0.md)). 1.2.0 plays with 1.0.0 and 1.1.0, but guest travel and carried keys need the host on 1.2.0, so update together.

New install: see the README (copy the two files next to `DARKSOULS.exe`, set a shared `Password`, rest at a bonfire for the co-op items). The co-op uses its own save, `DRAKS0005.co2`. Back up your saves.

**Online play: no guarantees.** The mod changes the game in memory; use at your own risk. No responsibility is accepted for bans or lost progress (see the README and LICENSE).

Send `PTDE-FPS-Unlock.log` from every player in the session, and any `*-crash.dmp`, with a bug report.
