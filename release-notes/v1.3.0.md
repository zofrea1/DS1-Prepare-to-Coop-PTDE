# Prepare to Co-op Edition v1.3.0

NPC summons that everyone sees, NPC voice lines everyone near them hears, one-time drops and boss rewards that reach every player, a Homeward Bone and Darksign for guests too, and a much faster network. 1.3.0 plays with 1.0.0, 1.1.0 and 1.2.0 (same protocol); most of what is new needs the players involved on 1.3.0, so update together.

**Includes the [DS1 PTDE FPS Unlock](https://github.com/zofrea1/DS1-FPS-Unlock-PTDE-Remastered) 1.9.0**: a choice of where the frame cap waits (`FPSLimitPoint`), the start-up logos skipped by default, steadier steep slides, slide steering, smooth in-engine cutscene cameras and the soul counter at the right speed.

## What's new

### Network
- **Up to ten times the updates.** `NetworkTickRate` (default 30, up to 60; the game sends 6 a second) sets how often every game of the session sends where its player and the enemies it moves are. Other players and enemies are drawn much closer to where they really are. The host's setting is used by the whole session.
- **Smoothing** (`NetworkSmoothing`, default 33 ms): other players and the enemies other games move are drawn a margin behind their newest update, so a late update does not make them jerk. Fast enemies stay smooth at high tick rates.

### NPCs
- **NPC summons in a session.** The host's summoned NPC phantoms (Solaire and the other white signs) appear for every guest and invader, also for a player who joins later or arrives somewhere else, and fight the invader. They take none of the four places.
- **NPC voice lines are heard by everyone near the NPC**, with their subtitles: conversations, and what an NPC says when hit or killed, reach every player within 30 m, not only the one whose game made them speak. Each listener hears the conversation at its own pace, once.
- **NPC gifts** reach a player whose game was loading when the NPC handed them out.
- **The Cursed Pendant** brings the NPC invaders (Kirk, Leeroy, Mildred, Jeremiah) even to a hollow player.
- Oscar's scripted death in the Undead Asylum waits until no player is talking to him.

### Loot and rewards
- **One-time drops reach every player.** A drop the world gives only once (a Black Knight's, a crystal lizard's, a Titanite Demon's, a mimic's, Pinwheel's masks, the Stray Demon's slab, a boss's rewards) reaches everyone in the session. A player whose game was loading, far away or down when it died gets their own roll a few seconds later, straight into the inventory, so no death, team wipe or trip home takes it first.
- **Boss rewards reach a player who fell** in the fight: their respawn waits for the rewards. Invaders take none of them (neither the clear bonus souls nor the event's items).
- Items the mod gives (the co-op items at a rest, a Crimson Blossom, an invasion's Humanity, a missed drop) show in the game's own item-get window.

### Travel
- **Guests can use the Homeward Bone, the Darksign and the Homeward miracle.** Each takes everyone to the host's last bonfire, as the host's own does, and waits for the host to close any menu.
- **The Homeward Bone costs every co-op player a bone**, so it can be used only when everyone holds one, and as in single player it works in a boss fight, taking everyone out of it.
- **The Darksign costs everyone's souls and humanity** and can always be used: in a boss fight, while a teammate is down, and during an invasion, where it sends the invaders home first.
- **The Homeward miracle** can be cast in a session by the host or a guest, whenever the bone could be used, and costs nobody a bone.

### Boss fights and enemies
- **An attack is never cut short when an enemy changes hands.** Each enemy is moved by one game, normally the one whose player is nearest. A change of hands let the new game's AI start a different attack at once, part way through the one under way (the Asylum Demon broke off swings and once its jumping slam in mid-air). An attack an enemy has begun now plays to its natural end before another game takes it over. Targets, turning and the AI's choices are the game's own.
- **Guests use the host's scaling settings**, so every game in a world gives an enemy the same health.
- **Posture scaling** (`EnemyPostureScaling`, `BossPostureScaling`) works; it was not applied before. New defaults: enemies 45 % health and 30 % posture per extra player, bosses 125 % and 40 %.
- An enemy keeps its share of health when the scaling changes (a player joins or leaves); the second Bell Gargoyle comes in at its own share. A boss whose fight has not begun in a game no longer passes that game's "health at most X %" tests, which brought the second gargoyle in during a Hellkite fight far away.
- Event animations an event waits for (a boss's entry, a lift) follow the game that moves the character, so an event no longer waits forever on a copy that never plays it.

### Invasions
- **Invaders of one world are a team.** An invader who falls while another still stands spectates them, and they win or lose together.
- **InvasionCooldown** (default 3 minutes, up to 10): after an invasion of a world ends, no other invader can come in for that long.
- **An invader who cannot get in is told why**: a world on cooldown (with the time left), a boss fight, or a full world. A connection that falls through is tried once more by itself.
- **Boss fog sealing is a choice** (`SealBossFogsDuringInvasions`, default off): off, boss fogs work as in the game (the host's entry sends the invader home); on, they stay shut for the host and the guests while an invader is in the world, as in 1.1.0 and 1.2.0.

### Crimson Blossoms
- They wither when anyone in the session rests, on a team wipe, and when the session ends: a guest's as they go home, a host's when they close the world. The health they took comes back each time, as at a rest.

### Other
- **Update check.** At each start the mod looks for a newer release on GitHub and says so on the title screen (`CheckForUpdates`).
- **The settings file is now `PTDE-Coop-FPS-Unlock.ini`.** The old `PTDE-FPS-Unlock.ini` is still read when the new one is not there.

## Fixes

- **Crash on joining** a host whose NPC phantom stood in a map not loaded for the guest.
- **A cutscene skipped by one player** (the Undead Asylum's crow) no longer gives a slower player a loading screen and a warp: their own cutscene finishes the trip, with the landing and the hint on arrival.
- **The host could turn invisible** for a guest after a few deaths (their character was never rebuilt).
- **A guest's Crimson Blossoms** vanished after a load in the host's world, and one could follow them home as if it were a key.
- **Nameplates**: a player could show their Steam name or "[unknown]" instead of their character's name, or nothing at all (no name, soul level or ping) when the game's own record of them was empty.
- **A lost lobby** after a network drop is remade, so the world can be found again.
- **A guest's bloodstain** could be written over with 0 souls while their respawn waited.
- **Enemy health** could differ between games for an enemy far from a player.

## New and changed settings

New: `NetworkTickRate` (30), `NetworkSmoothing` (33), `InvasionCooldown` (3), `SealBossFogsDuringInvasions` (false), `CheckForUpdates` (true). Changed defaults: `EnemyHealthScaling` 35 → 45, `EnemyPostureScaling` 15 → 30, `BossHealthScaling` 100 → 125, `BossPostureScaling` 20 → 40. From the FPS Unlock 1.9.0: `FPSLimitPoint` (early), `FixSlideContact`, `FixSlideSteer`, `FixCutsceneCamera`, `FixSoulCounter` (all true), and `SkipIntro` is now true by default.

## Upgrading

Replace `xinput1_3.dll` and use the new `PTDE-Coop-FPS-Unlock.ini` (copy your `Password` and any settings you changed into it; your old `PTDE-FPS-Unlock.ini` can then be deleted). A missing key takes its default, so an old INI also works. 1.3.0 plays with 1.0.0, 1.1.0 and 1.2.0, but the network rate, NPC summons and voice lines, one-time drops, guest Homeward items, invader teams and the cooldown need the players involved on 1.3.0, so update together.

New install: see the README (copy the two files next to `DARKSOULS.exe`, set a shared `Password`, rest at a bonfire for the co-op items). The co-op uses its own save, `DRAKS0005.co2`. Back up your saves.

**Online play: no guarantees.** The mod changes the game in memory; use at your own risk. No responsibility is accepted for bans or lost progress (see the README and LICENSE).

Send `PTDE-FPS-Unlock.log` from every player in the session, and any `*-crash.dmp`, with a bug report.
