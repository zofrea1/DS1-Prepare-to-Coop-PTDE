# Prepare to Co-op Edition v1.4.0 (draft)

Smoothing that follows each player's connection, scripted set pieces that every player sees, fast enemies that stay
smooth at high tick rates, and the first step to sessions of up to eight players. 1.4.0 plays with 1.0.0 to 1.3.0
(same protocol); what is new needs the players involved on 1.4.0.

## What's new

### Network
- **Dynamic smoothing** (`DynamicSmoothing`, on by default). Every other player's character, and every enemy another
  player's game moves, gets its own smoothing from how evenly its updates arrive: down to 16 ms on a steady connection,
  up to 200 ms on a poor one (players across the world, 200 ms pings, Wi-Fi). It rises quickly when updates start
  coming late and falls slowly once they are steady again. `NetworkSmoothing` is where it starts; `DynamicSmoothing =
  false` keeps `NetworkSmoothing` for everyone.
- **The floating health bar shows it**: `OverheadPlayerDisplay = 5` (the new default) adds "lerp 48ms", the smoothing
  that player is drawn with in your game, after the ping.
- **Fast enemies stay smooth at high tick rates.** An enemy's copy is drawn a little behind its updates on purpose;
  the test that makes a copy jump to an update now allows for that, so the fastest movers (the Taurus Demon bounding
  down the bridge at 60 updates a second) run instead of sliding.

### World
- **Scripted set pieces play for the player who set them off.** A flight, leap or landing an event plays on an enemy
  is now moved by the game of the player whose step began it, until it ends, and every other game follows. The
  Hellkite's first flight over the Undead Burg's bridge no longer goes missing when the host is far away, and a boss's
  scripted leap is never handed to another game halfway through.
- **Bosses the game keeps with the host stay with the host.** The Taurus Demon, the Hellkites, the Moonlight
  Butterfly, the Sanctuary Guardian, Artorias, Manus, Pinwheel and a few others are moved by the host's game for the
  whole fight in the game's own co-op. Since guests run the world's events, every game had claimed them alike and they
  changed hands between players mid-fight, breaking off whatever they were doing. A guest's game now bids for them as
  the game's guest does (a host who falls, or is far away, still hands them over).
- **No more sudden turns when a boss changes hands.** Since 1.3.0 a boss that passed to another player's game was
  turned on that player at once, which made bosses snap around in fights with nothing wrong. A boss now keeps the
  target its own AI chose; only one that has had no target for 4 seconds while a player stands near it in its fight
  (the second Bell Gargoyle after the host fell) is pointed at that player.

### Players
- **Up to eight players** (`MaxPlayers`, experimental, 4 by default): the host, guests and invaders together, NPC
  summons on top. Above four every player in the session needs 1.4.0 and `MaxPlayers` set high enough. Every other
  player gets a floating health bar (the game's HUD has seven); NPC summons have none while four or more players are
  in the session. See docs/MORE-PLAYERS.md.
