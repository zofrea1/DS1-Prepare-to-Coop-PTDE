# Prepare to Co-op Edition v1.5.0

(In progress.)

## What's new

- **The Death Knell**, a new co-op item, given at every bonfire rest like the others. Ring it in a session to spectate the other co-op players (D-pad left/right or the arrow keys to choose, B or Esc to stop), and ring it again to go to the one you are watching: at once when they are in your area, through a load when they are farther away. You arrive the way a summoned phantom does, with one of the game's summon arrivals at random (Solaire's "Praise the Sun" among them), and the bell takes its price: one soul level, never below your class's starting level ("! SOUL LEVEL DRAINED !"). The level taken is the last one you gained since 1.5.0, or one of your levels at random for those gained before (the game does not record the order). Not usable in a boss fight, yours or theirs.
