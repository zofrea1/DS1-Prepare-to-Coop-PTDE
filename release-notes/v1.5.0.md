# Prepare to Co-op Edition v1.5.0

The Death Knell and the Vigil Soapstone, to go to each other anywhere in the world; enemies scaled for the players near them, and harder by default; the Bloody Mary, a death debuff the whole session shares; and spectating that shows a far teammate's area as they see it. 1.5.0 plays with 1.0.0 to 1.4.0 (same protocol); what is new needs the players involved on 1.5.0, so update together.

**Includes the [DS1 PTDE FPS Unlock](https://github.com/zofrea1/DS1-FPS-Unlock-PTDE-Remastered) 1.9.0**, as 1.4.0 did.

## What's new

### Going to each other
- **The Death Knell**, a new co-op item, given at every bonfire rest like the others. Ring it in a session to spectate the other co-op players and their summon signs (D-pad left/right or the arrow keys to choose, B or Esc to stop). You stay down in the bell's crouch while the view is open, and the players around you see it; a hit lands and closes the view. Press the action button on "Summon yourself to ..." (or A on the pad) to go to the one you are watching: you rise with the bell's light and go, at once when they are in your area, through a load when they are farther away. You arrive as a summoned phantom does, in its white glow or with Solaire's "Praise the Sun", at random. Then the bell takes its price ("! DEBUFFED !"): your poise breaks with a riposte's stab, you get a Crimson Blossom, everyone in the session gets a Bloody Mary (the enemies grow hardier), and you lose every soul and all humanity you carry, as to the Darksign. The Blossom and the Bloody Mary are the price with `DeathDebuffs` or `BloodyMary` off too: those settings are about deaths. `DeathKnellSoulLevel` takes one soul level instead of the souls and humanity, never below your class's starting level ("! SOUL LEVEL DRAINED !"; the level taken is the last one you gained since 1.5.0, or one at random for those gained before). Not usable in a boss fight, yours or theirs, nor to go to a teammate in a cutscene or a load.
- **The Vigil Soapstone**, another new co-op item, given at every bonfire rest: in a session it leaves your summon sign where you stand (free, one at a time), drawn in every co-op player's world. The others' Death Knells list it with the co-op players ("Summon yourself to <name>'s summon sign"), and going to it costs only the Crimson Blossom and the Bloody Mary: it pays to set a warp point for the others. The White Sign Soapstone stays the game's own.
- **Guests arrive as summoned phantoms do** when they join a session: one of the same two arrivals plays in place of the game's own rise at the host's bonfire, on every player's screen.

### Enemies
- **Enemies scaled for the players near them** (`VicinityScaling`, on by default; the host's setting decides). Each enemy counts the co-op players whose game has it active (the same test that decides who gets a kill's souls), not everyone in the session: two players in the Undead Burg and two in the Catacombs each fight enemies scaled for two, where the whole world was scaled for four. An enemy's scaling follows the players as they come and go, and it keeps its share of health when it changes. Every co-op player needs 1.5.0; with an older one in the session, the whole session counts as before.
- **Harder enemies by default**, for the Death Knell and the Vigil Soapstone's strength and for sessions of up to eight: `EnemyHealthScaling` 50 (was 45), `EnemyPostureScaling` 35 (was 30) and `BossHealthScaling` 150 (was 125) per extra player. An INI you already have keeps its own values.

### Death debuffs
- **The Bloody Mary** (`BloodyMary`, on by default), a second death debuff alongside the Crimson Blossom: every co-op player's death gives everyone in the session a Bloody Mary (up to five), and each makes the world's enemies hardier: a third of `EnemyHealthScaling` more health for regular enemies, an eighth of `BossHealthScaling` for bosses, added on top of the health a living enemy has. They wither for everyone when anyone rests at a bonfire or the whole team falls. Either debuff, both or neither can be on.

### Spectating
- **A far teammate's area as they see it** (`SpectateStreaming`, on by default). Following a teammate, after you fall in a boss fight or with the Death Knell, your game now loads their map when it had not and draws the world from where they stand, across map boundaries too: the whole area around them, the enemies there moving as in their game, their area's colour grading and lighting, and the light the game keeps around a player. Before, the view had only what your game had loaded around you: a teammate in another area stood in a world with parts missing, without the enemies around them and in your area's light, or was not drawn at all.

### Players and sessions
- **Players reappear sooner after a death or a load.** A player who respawned or arrived could take five seconds to show up in the others' games after they stood; they now come in much sooner.
- **Send your logs to the developer** (`UploadLogs`, off by default): when the developer asks you to, your game's log goes up to the address they give you (`UploadLogsTo`) when the game closes, filed under your PC's name or the name you choose (`UploadLogsName`), to help find and fix problems. A log a crash left behind goes at the next start, and waits for a later start while the address can't be reached (the newest five are kept). Nothing is sent unless you turn it on and give an address.

## Fixes

- **A prompt stuck on screen** ("Rest at bonfire" and the like) after a teleport or a load: the Death Knell, the Homeward Bone, the Darksign, the Homeward miracle and travel left the prompt of what stood near the player until another of its kind replaced it.
- **Enemies far from you healed when you came near them** in a session: an enemy your game had not been updating got the co-op health scaling from the mod, and the game added the same growth again once it updated the enemy.
- **An enemy's health bar filled for a moment when a co-op player joined or left**: the enemy was given its new scaling anew, stood at its own max for a frame and grew back whole, until its share of health was put back half a second later (the other players' games took the full bar meanwhile). The scaling now changes in place.
