# Prepare to Co-op Edition v1.5.0

(In progress.)

## What's new

- **The Death Knell**, a new co-op item, given at every bonfire rest like the others. Ring it in a session to spectate the other co-op players (D-pad left/right or the arrow keys to choose, B or Esc to stop), and press the action button on "Summon yourself to ..." to go to the one you are watching: at once when they are in your area, through a load when they are farther away. You arrive the way a summoned phantom does, with one of the game's summon arrivals at random (Solaire's "Praise the Sun" among them). Then the bell takes its price ("! DEBUFFED !"): your poise breaks with a riposte's stab, you get a Crimson Blossom, everyone in the session gets a Bloody Mary (the enemies grow hardier), and you lose every soul and all humanity you carry, as to the Darksign. `DeathKnellSoulLevel` takes one soul level instead of the souls and humanity, never below your class's starting level ("! SOUL LEVEL DRAINED !"; the level taken is the last one you gained since 1.5.0, or one at random for those gained before). Not usable in a boss fight, yours or theirs.
- **The Vigil Soapstone**, another new co-op item, given at every bonfire rest: in a session it leaves your summon sign where you stand (free, one at a time). The others' Death Knells list it with the co-op players ("Summon yourself to <name>'s summon sign"), and going to it costs only the Crimson Blossom and the Bloody Mary: it pays to set a warp point for the others.
- **Guests arrive as summoned phantoms do** when they join a session (one of the same arrivals).
- **The Bloody Mary** (`BloodyMary`, on by default), a second death debuff alongside the Crimson Blossom: every co-op player's death gives everyone in the session a Bloody Mary (up to five), and each makes the world's enemies hardier: a third of `EnemyHealthScaling` more health for regular enemies, an eighth of `BossHealthScaling` for bosses, added on top of the health a living enemy has. They wither for everyone when anyone rests at a bonfire or the whole team falls. Either debuff, both or neither can be on.

## Fixes

- **A prompt stuck on screen** ("Rest at bonfire" and the like) after a teleport or a load: the Death Knell, the Homeward Bone, the Darksign, the Homeward miracle and travel left the prompt of what stood near the player until another of its kind replaced it.
- **Enemies far from you healed when you came near them** in a session: an enemy your game had not been updating got the co-op health scaling from the mod, and the game added the same growth again once it updated the enemy.
- **An enemy's health bar filled for a moment when a co-op player joined or left**: the enemy was given its new scaling anew, stood at its own max for a frame and grew back whole, until its share of health was put back half a second later (the other players' games took the full bar meanwhile). The scaling now changes in place.
