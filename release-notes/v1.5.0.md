# Prepare to Co-op Edition v1.5.0

(In progress.)

## What's new

- **The Death Knell**, a new co-op item, given at every bonfire rest like the others. Ring it in a session to spectate the other co-op players (D-pad left/right or the arrow keys to choose, B or Esc to stop), and press the action button on "Summon yourself to ..." to go to the one you are watching: at once when they are in your area, through a load when they are farther away. You arrive the way a summoned phantom does, with one of the game's summon arrivals at random (Solaire's "Praise the Sun" among them). Then the bell takes its price: your poise breaks with a riposte's stab, and you lose one soul level, never below your class's starting level ("! SOUL LEVEL DRAINED !"). The level taken is the last one you gained since 1.5.0, or one of your levels at random for those gained before (the game does not record the order). Not usable in a boss fight, yours or theirs.
- **Guests arrive as summoned phantoms do** when they join a session (one of the same arrivals).
- **The Bloody Mary** (`BloodyMary`, on by default), a second death debuff alongside the Crimson Blossom: every co-op player's death gives everyone in the session a Bloody Mary (up to five), and each makes the world's enemies hardier: a third of `EnemyHealthScaling` more health for regular enemies, an eighth of `BossHealthScaling` for bosses, added on top of the health a living enemy has. They wither for everyone when anyone rests at a bonfire or the whole team falls. Either debuff, both or neither can be on.

## Fixes

- **A prompt stuck on screen** ("Rest at bonfire" and the like) after a teleport or a load: the Death Knell, the Homeward Bone, the Darksign, the Homeward miracle and travel left the prompt of what stood near the player until another of its kind replaced it.
