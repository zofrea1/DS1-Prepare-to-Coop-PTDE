# Prepare to Co-op Edition v1.5.1

Bug fixes for 1.5.0. **Plays with 1.5.0** (the co-op protocol is the same), but the Demon Ruins lift fix needs every player in that map to have 1.5.1.

**Includes the [DS1 PTDE FPS Unlock](https://github.com/zofrea1/DS1-FPS-Unlock-PTDE-Remastered) 1.9.0**, as 1.5.0 did.

## Fixes

- **A Homeward Bone used in a session took the whole stack.** One bone used by anyone took every Homeward Bone each player carried (all 99 of a full stack), where it should take one from each. Now each player loses one bone per trip, and the last one leaves the quick bar as the game's own use does. Bones in the Bottomless Box were never touched.
- **The Demon Ruins lift went back and forth and jumped between its ends** (the lift that starts working once the Centipede Demon is dead) with more than one player in that area. Every player's game moved it toward its own player, so two players at different ends sent it both ways at once. The lift's position could also reach a game before the call that moved it, which then sent the lift the other way, and the next stop put it back at the far end. The lift now moves for one game at a time (the host's when the host is in the Demon Ruins, else one guest's) and answers every player in the area the same way: anyone can still call it or ride it, and it stays where it is for everyone. A player alone in the Demon Ruins moves it as before.
