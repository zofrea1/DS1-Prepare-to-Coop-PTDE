# Prepare to Co-op Edition v1.6.0

Group invasions and an invader limit, with every fix of 1.5.1. **Plays with 1.5.0 and 1.5.1** (the co-op protocol is the same). Group invasions need 1.6.0 for everyone in the group and for the world they invade.

**Includes the [DS1 PTDE FPS Unlock](https://github.com/zofrea1/DS1-FPS-Unlock-PTDE-Remastered) 1.9.0**, as 1.5.0 did.

## New

- **Group invasions.** Host a world, with guests or alone, and use the Chaos Eye Orb (or Ctrl+F4): the whole party invades another world together.
  - Your world stays open while the search runs, to co-op players and to invaders: a search is no shelter from invasion. A host alone stays open to invaders too.
  - The search only takes a world with room for all of you under its invader limit, and that world's host holds your places for 45 seconds. Places your group hasn't filled by then are open to any invader again.
  - Only then does your world stop taking invaders, and it closes. Your guests go home first and everyone invades that world, as one team of evil spirits that wins or loses together.
  - When the invasion is over and you're home, your world opens again and your guests are brought back to it, arriving at your last bonfire.
  - Use the orb again to call the search off.
  - An invasion of your world comes first, so two parties after a fight get it: an invader who comes in during the search calls it off, and so does another group your world is holding places for. Neither lets a new search start until that invasion is over.
  - It also can't start or go on during a boss fight, while the party travels, or while you or a guest of the group is down or still on their way in.
- **An invader limit** (`ExtraInvaders`). A world takes at most as many invaders at once as it has co-op players (the host and the guests), plus one: a player alone meets two invaders at most, a party of three four. Raise `ExtraInvaders` to let larger groups of invaders in; it can't go below one. `MaxPlayers` still counts everyone together. The limit holds for invaders of every version, and the searches of 1.6.0 pass over a world that already has as many as it takes.
- The Chaos Eye Orb's description says what a host's use does.

## Fixes

- **An invader's Death Knell to a far summon sign broke the invasion on the others' screens.** When the trip went by a load (the sign was in a part of the world the invader's game hadn't loaded), the invader arrived without their red phantom glow, and the host and the guests saw them die and vanish, while in their own game they were alive and could still fight. The load turned the invader back into their own body, and the others' games held their character dead after a moment of 0 HP that the mod's check missed. The invader now keeps their phantom form through the load, and a character the game holds dead while its player never died is made again (once: a character made again stays as it is). The bell's knockdown animation had nothing to do with it.
- **A join made within ten seconds of leaving another session no longer falls through.** When a session's other players leave, the game starts a ten-second timer to end a session left with one player, and nothing reset it when a new join began: a join started within those ten seconds was ended at the tenth second, before its loading screen. It hit the second player of every group invasion (they had just left their host's world) and the first invasion after an invasion ended. The timer is now stopped when a join begins or a world is opened. Should the game still end a join before its load, the mod knocks again once the game's leave is over.
- **Faster first contact.** The first message between two players could wait about three seconds for Steam to set up their link (a group's second invader's knock took 3.3 s to arrive, the first's 12 ms). A world that holds a group's places now lets each of them in at once, and every game lets in a player of its lobby the moment Steam asks, without waiting for Steam's word that they entered it. A world that doesn't answer a group's ask in time is reported as "not answering" instead of "loading or fallen".
- **Rewards for a whole team of invaders come as one.** The host and guests who defeat several invaders got a separate "Reward: 1 Humanity" for each, one over the other, so two looked like one. They now come together: "The invaders are defeated. Reward: 2 Humanity".
- **No item window for an item that is already gone.** A fallen host's Bloody Mary was shown after the team wipe that had taken it away, beside "YOUR TEAM DIED". An item that is no longer held by the time its window would show is not shown.
- **Joins wait less.** Every wait of the mod's own around a join is about a quarter of what it was, for guests and invaders alike: a world that answers "busy" (loading, or its player down) is asked again after 0.75 s instead of 3 s, with as much patience as before when it is the only world, and a knock with no answer is sent again after 5 s instead of 20 s. A search that gets no answer from Steam tries again after 2 s and gives up after 5 s. A lobby that doesn't let you in gives way to the next world after 5 s. A join that never gets under way gives up after 12 s instead of 40 s. A finished invader the game doesn't send home is sent home after 2.5 s instead of 10 s. A missing character of another player is asked for again after 1.25 s instead of 5 s. A group's players knock and come back a fraction of a second after they stand, and a host whose group leaves closes its world in at most 1.5 s. The game's own part of a join (the few seconds before the loading screen, and the load) is the game's and takes what it takes.

## Fixes (from 1.5.1)

- **A Homeward Bone used in a session took the whole stack.** One bone used by anyone took every Homeward Bone each player carried (all 99 of a full stack), where it should take one from each. Now each player loses one bone per trip, and the last one leaves the quick bar as the game's own use does. Bones in the Bottomless Box were never touched.
- **The Demon Ruins lift went back and forth and jumped between its ends** (the lift that starts working once the Centipede Demon is dead) with more than one player in that area. The lift now moves for one game at a time (the host's when the host is in the Demon Ruins, else one guest's) and answers every player in the area the same way: anyone can still call it or ride it, and it stays where it is for everyone. Every player in that map needs 1.5.1 or later.
- **An invader's game no longer moves the world's enemies.** As in vanilla, enemies take no notice of an invader, and the co-op players fight them in their own games. An invader's game could take over enemies far from the invader, especially around a co-op player watched with the Death Knell, and then showed them attacking that player while nothing touched them in their own game: they looked invincible.
- **An invader's Death Knell to a far summon sign took the key items they held** for the rest of the invasion. They now come back once the invader stands at the sign.
