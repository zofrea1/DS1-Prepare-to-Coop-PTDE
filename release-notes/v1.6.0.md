# Prepare to Co-op Edition v1.6.0

Group invasions and an invader limit, with every fix of 1.5.1. **Plays with 1.5.0 and 1.5.1** (the co-op protocol is the same). Group invasions need 1.6.0 for everyone in the group and for the world they invade.

**Includes the [DS1 PTDE FPS Unlock](https://github.com/zofrea1/DS1-FPS-Unlock-PTDE-Remastered) 1.9.0**, as 1.5.0 did.

## New

- **Group invasions.** Host a world, with guests or alone, and use the Chaos Eye Orb (or Ctrl+F4): the whole party invades another world together.
  - Your world stays open while the search runs. Co-op players can still come in, but invaders can't find your world meanwhile, so none is in it when you go.
  - The search only takes a world with room for all of you under its invader limit, and that world's host holds your places for two minutes.
  - Then your world closes. Your guests go home first and everyone invades that world, as one team of evil spirits that wins or loses together.
  - When the invasion is over and you're home, your world opens again and your guests are brought back to it, arriving at your last bonfire.
  - Use the orb again to call the search off.
  - Not while an invader is in your world, during a boss fight, while the party travels, or while a guest is still on their way in or down.
- **An invader limit** (`ExtraInvaders`). A world takes at most as many invaders at once as it has co-op players (the host and the guests), plus one: a player alone meets two invaders at most, a party of three four. Raise `ExtraInvaders` to let larger groups of invaders in; it can't go below one. `MaxPlayers` still counts everyone together. The limit holds for invaders of every version, and the searches of 1.6.0 pass over a world that already has as many as it takes.
- The Chaos Eye Orb's description says what a host's use does.

## Fixes (from 1.5.1)

- **A Homeward Bone used in a session took the whole stack.** One bone used by anyone took every Homeward Bone each player carried (all 99 of a full stack), where it should take one from each. Now each player loses one bone per trip, and the last one leaves the quick bar as the game's own use does. Bones in the Bottomless Box were never touched.
- **The Demon Ruins lift went back and forth and jumped between its ends** (the lift that starts working once the Centipede Demon is dead) with more than one player in that area. The lift now moves for one game at a time (the host's when the host is in the Demon Ruins, else one guest's) and answers every player in the area the same way: anyone can still call it or ride it, and it stays where it is for everyone. Every player in that map needs 1.5.1 or later.
- **An invader's game no longer moves the world's enemies.** As in vanilla, enemies take no notice of an invader, and the co-op players fight them in their own games. An invader's game could take over enemies far from the invader, especially around a co-op player watched with the Death Knell, and then showed them attacking that player while nothing touched them in their own game: they looked invincible.
- **An invader's Death Knell to a far summon sign took the key items they held** for the rest of the invasion. They now come back once the invader stands at the sign.
