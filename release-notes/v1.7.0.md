# Prepare to Co-op Edition v1.7.0

Fairer invasions and more warnings for the invaded. **Plays with 1.5.0, 1.5.1 and 1.6.0** (the co-op protocol is the same); each new feature needs 1.7.0 for the players it involves (see below).

**Includes the [DS1 PTDE FPS Unlock](https://github.com/zofrea1/DS1-FPS-Unlock-PTDE-Remastered) 2.1.0**: kicks, jumping attacks, roll direction and the switchback read the stick as at 30 FPS, Blighttown's swamp and lingering attacks such as Poison Mist build up at the original rate, and bodies and enemies are no longer flung when they load in (see its [release notes](https://github.com/zofrea1/DS1-FPS-Unlock-PTDE-Remastered/releases/tag/v2.1.0-PTDE-DX9)).

## New

- **Level matching for invasions.** An invader's search prefers worlds with a player near the invader's soul level: a world with a player in the invader's range (10% below their level to 20 + 10% above it, the original game's Red Eye Orb range) is 8 times as likely to be picked, the pull fading with every level outside it. It is a preference only: a world far from your level is still invaded when it is the only one, and invasion cooldowns are kept. A group goes by its highest soul level. Needs 1.7.0 for the invader and the world's host.
- **Dried Fingers draw harder.** A world with Dried Fingers is 150% more likely to be picked (was 65%), on top of the level matching.
- **YOUR TEAM DIED for invaders.** When two or more invaders are in a world, as a group or one by one, and all of them fall, they all see YOUR TEAM DIED. An invader alone still sees YOU DIED. Needs 1.7.0 for the invaders.
- **A warning when a group invades.** As the world lets in the first player of a group invasion, its host and every guest see "Beware! A band of N evil spirits is breaking into this world!". Needs 1.7.0 for the host (and each guest to see it).
- **Spied on by the Death Knell.** A co-op player watched by an invader's Death Knell sees "Your Death Knell tolls uneasily... an invader is spying on you!", at most once a minute however many invaders watch. Needs 1.7.0 for the invader, the host and the player watched.

## Install

Copy the files next to `DARKSOULS.exe` (`dxvk_d3d9.dll` only if you want to try DXVK). Upgrading from 1.6.0: replace `xinput1_3.dll`; your `PTDE-Coop-FPS-Unlock.ini` keeps working, and the FPS Unlock's new switches are on even when it does not list them. Everyone sets the same `Password`. Back up your saves; co-op characters are kept in their own save file.
