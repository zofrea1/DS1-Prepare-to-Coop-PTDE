# Prepare to Co-op Edition v1.8.0

Higher frame rates in busy places, DXVK with G-SYNC, and the Parchment of Deliverance retired. **Plays with 1.5.0 to 1.7.0** (the co-op protocol is the same).

**Includes the [DS1 PTDE FPS Unlock](https://github.com/zofrea1/DS1-FPS-Unlock-PTDE-Remastered) 2.2.0** (see its [release notes](https://github.com/zofrea1/DS1-FPS-Unlock-PTDE-Remastered/releases/tag/v2.2.0-PTDE-DX9)):

- **Higher frame rates where the game's draw thread was the limit**: the same picture with less of its overhead (`AsyncReadback`, `FilterSamplerStates`, `FastEffects`, `FastRenderMath`, `FastReadLocks`, `ReuseSurfaces`). On a Ryzen 7 5800X3D and an RTX 3090 at 4K: Darkroot Garden 79 to 157-171 FPS, Blighttown 58-73 to 96 (129 with DXVK), the Undead Burg bridge 45-125 to 155-168.
- **No more slowdown while standing still** with fewer than four controllers.
- **No more hitch every 5 seconds** (`SteamPauseGuard`). On Windows the Steam client pauses about every 5 s to check Wi-Fi, and every Steam call made meanwhile waits; the game and co-op make several every frame. The game's now go only right after Steam has answered quickly, the lobby filter it sets every frame waits for the next lobby search, and the game's and co-op's packets are sent (co-op's also read) on threads of their own, where Steam's pause holds back packets, not frames.
- **A winning invader leaves at once.** After the last co-op player fell, the invader waited for the game's own timer (about 20 s) and could meet the host and guests again at their bonfire after their team's reload and hit them. Now they go home 8 seconds after the victory, or as soon as the host's world starts reloading.
- **The "lerp" on a player's health bar disappears again** when that player has stood still for 3 seconds (a host whose only other player stood still kept showing it).
- **DXVK: G-SYNC / FreeSync works** while Windows HDR is off (`DxvkExclusiveFullscreen = auto`). It needs exclusive fullscreen, and with HDR on Windows would switch HDR off for as long as the game holds it, so then DXVK keeps HDR and runs without variable refresh (Direct3D 9 has both). `true` takes exclusive fullscreen anyway.
- **DXVK colors with Windows HDR**: NVIDIA's RTX HDR, if it is on for the game in the NVIDIA app, applies only under DXVK and crushes the shadows; turn it off for the game for the same picture as Direct3D 9. The log says when it is active.

## Changed

- **The Parchment of Deliverance is retired.** It is no longer given at a bonfire, and one you carry is taken out of your inventory (one in the Bottomless Box goes when you take it out). It was a holdover from Seamless Co-op's NPC repair; an NPC lost while visiting another world never came up in play.

## Install

Copy the files next to `DARKSOULS.exe` (`dxvk_d3d9.dll` only if you want to try DXVK). Upgrading from 1.7.0: replace `xinput1_3.dll`; your `PTDE-Coop-FPS-Unlock.ini` keeps working, and the FPS Unlock's new switches are on even when it does not list them. Everyone sets the same `Password`. Back up your saves; co-op characters are kept in their own save file.
