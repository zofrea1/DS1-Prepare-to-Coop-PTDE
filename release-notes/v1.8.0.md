# Prepare to Co-op Edition v1.8.0

Higher frame rates, no more hitch every 5 seconds, a winning invader who goes home on time, and the Parchment of Deliverance retired. **Plays with 1.5.0 to 1.7.0** (the co-op protocol is the same).

**Includes the [DS1 PTDE FPS Unlock](https://github.com/zofrea1/DS1-FPS-Unlock-PTDE-Remastered) 2.2.0** (see its [release notes](https://github.com/zofrea1/DS1-FPS-Unlock-PTDE-Remastered/releases/tag/v2.2.0-PTDE-DX9)):

- **Higher frame rates where the game's draw thread was the limit**, with the same picture (only the eye adaptation, about a second long, starts two frames later). On a Ryzen 7 5800X3D and an RTX 3090 at 4K: Darkroot Garden 79 to 157-171 FPS, Blighttown 58-73 to 96 (129 with DXVK), the Undead Burg bridge 45-125 to 155-168.
- **No more hitch every 5 seconds** (`SteamPauseGuard`). On Windows the Steam client checks Wi-Fi about every 5 s, and every Steam call made meanwhile waits (15 ms on a PC with Wi-Fi); the game and co-op made several every frame. Now the game's every-frame calls go only right after Steam has answered quickly, and the game's and co-op's packets go through Steam on threads of their own, where Steam's pause holds back packets, not frames. Co-op's packets reach the game about 2 ms later than before on average. The Steam Deck has no such pause, and nothing is held back there.
- **No more slowdown while standing still** with fewer than four controllers.
- **DXVK** (experimental, off by default): G-SYNC / FreeSync works while Windows HDR is off (`DxvkExclusiveFullscreen = auto`); NVIDIA's RTX HDR, if it is on for the game in the NVIDIA app, applies only under DXVK and crushes the shadows (turn it off for the game; the log says when it is active); on the Steam Deck `DXVK = true` steps aside (Proton already runs the game on DXVK).

## Changed

- **A winning invader goes home 8 seconds after the victory**, or as soon as the host's world starts reloading. Before, the invader waited for the game's own timer (about 20 s), long enough to meet the host and guests again at their bonfire after their team's reload and hit them.
- **The Parchment of Deliverance is retired.** It is no longer given at a bonfire, and one you carry is taken out of your inventory (one in the Bottomless Box goes when you take it out). It was a holdover from Seamless Co-op's NPC repair; an NPC lost while visiting another world never came up in play.
- **A clearer INI.** The settings you are likely to change come first: the password, your world's rules (players, invaders, cooldown, boss fogs, rewards, shared drops), difficulty (Crimson Blossoms, Bloody Marys, the Death Knell's price, enemy scaling), the network rate and the health bars, then the frame rate cap and display. Below a line, the rest, set the way the mods are meant to be played. Your old INI keeps working: every setting is read wherever it stands.

## Fixed

- **The "lerp" on a player's health bar disappears again** when that player has stood still for 3 seconds (a host whose only other player stood still kept showing it).

## Install

Copy the files next to `DARKSOULS.exe` (`dxvk_d3d9.dll` only if you want to try DXVK). Upgrading from 1.7.0: replace `xinput1_3.dll` (and `PTDE-Coop-FPS-Unlock.ini` for the new layout, after noting your `Password` and anything else you changed; or keep yours: it works as it is, and the new switches are on when it does not list them). Everyone sets the same `Password`. Back up your saves; co-op characters are kept in their own save file.
