# Prepare to Co-op Edition v1.9.0

A new picture, invasions that no longer cost your progress, and every Prepare to Co-op player's ghosts and messages in your world. **Plays with 1.5.0 to 1.8.0** (the co-op protocol is the same); the shared ghosts, messages and bloodstains and Jolly cooperation need 1.9.0.

**Includes the [DS1 PTDE FPS Unlock](https://github.com/zofrea1/DS1-FPS-Unlock-PTDE-Remastered) 2.3.0** (see its [release notes](https://github.com/zofrea1/DS1-FPS-Unlock-PTDE-Remastered/releases/tag/v2.3.0-PTDE-DX9)):

- **The game at your resolution**, with its depth of field, glows, fog, colour and shadows redone to look the way they were made to look, without the blocks, banding and shimmer a high resolution exposed: a depth of field measured against the original, effects at half your resolution with the game's own upsample, 16-bit colour, a 4096 shadow map without the weave, 16x anisotropic filtering, smooth foliage edges with the game's anti-aliasing. All on by default; on a weaker PC lower `RenderResolution` or turn `HighPrecisionColor` off first.
- **DSfix is optional.** It stays fully supported (`YieldToDSFix = true` lets it decide resolution, anti-aliasing, depth of field, filtering and HUD scale), but this mod no longer needs it.
- **DXVK by default, with HDR and G-SYNC / FreeSync together** and frames paced to the game's steps.
- **Ultrawide screens**, the HUD and menus kept 16:9 on any screen, and **HUD scale** (`UIScale`, `UIScaleWorld`): the corner groups and boss bars, and the health bars and names over the other players and enemies.
- **Faster again** where the game's draw thread is the limit (Quelaag's Domain 138.5 to 179.5 FPS in its heaviest view), fewer loading hitches, and heat haze, effects that act at intervals and ragdolls that keep their timing at any frame rate.
- **Steam Deck: keep the game's anti-aliasing off.** With it on, grass and other fine detail show black specks; not solved yet.

## New

- **Invaders go home to where they stood** when the invasion was accepted, however it ends (victory, death, the host's Darksign, leaving), instead of at their last bonfire. Invading no longer costs your progress through an area. Your last bonfire stays your respawn point.
- **Group invasions: the guests come back to where they stood** in the host's world when the group went, instead of the host's bonfire, and the host goes home to where they stood too. A group can invade and then carry on through the area.
- **Invaders stay through area changes**: walking into another part of the map, or the game's own matching check for its covenant invasions, no longer sends an invader home.
- **Every Prepare to Co-op player's ghosts, messages and bloodstains**, whatever their password (players with 1.9.0). The game's own summoning and invading are off (soapstones, the eye orbs, the Dragon Eye, covenant summons): those items say so and are not used up. NPC summon signs and NPC invaders are unchanged.
- **Jolly cooperation** (`JollyCooperation`): two co-op players who Praise the Sun together (within 3 seconds and 10 m of each other) each get the warmth of Replenishment, 10 HP a second for a minute, once every 3 minutes.

## Fixed

- **A crash when a player's copy stood on ground that had unloaded** (an R6025 "pure virtual function call" error), for example an invader following players out of Blighttown.
- **The Death Knell works while poisoned or toxic**: every tick of a lasting effect counted as a hit and closed it.

## Install

Copy the files next to `DARKSOULS.exe`: `xinput1_3.dll`, `PTDE-Coop-FPS-Unlock.ini` and `dxvk_d3d9.dll` (DXVK is on by default). Upgrading from 1.8.0: replace `xinput1_3.dll` and `dxvk_d3d9.dll`, and keep your INI (it works as it is, and new settings take their defaults when it does not list them) or take the new one for its notes, after noting your `Password`. Everyone sets the same `Password`. Back up your saves; co-op characters are kept in their own save file. Steam Deck and Linux: launch option `WINEDLLOVERRIDES="xinput1_3=n,b" %command%`.

**Online play: no guarantees.** The mod modifies the game in memory. Use at your own risk; no responsibility is accepted for bans or lost progress (see the README and LICENSE).
