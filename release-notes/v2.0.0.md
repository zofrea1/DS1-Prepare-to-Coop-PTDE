# Prepare to Co-op Edition v2.0.0

Protection against the 2022 remote code execution exploit, invasion searches that say whose world was full and why, and the DS1 FPS Unlock 2.4.0 inside. **Plays with 1.5.0 to 1.9.0** (the co-op protocol is the same).

**Includes the [DS1 PTDE FPS Unlock](https://github.com/zofrea1/DS1-FPS-Unlock-PTDE-Remastered) 2.4.0** (see its [release notes](https://github.com/zofrea1/DS1-FPS-Unlock-PTDE-Remastered/releases/tag/v2.4.0-PTDE-DX9)):

- **Scripted sequences keep the original game's timing**: the waits in the game's event scripts (traps, doors, scripted attacks, a boss's entrance) end where they do at 30 FPS. The Undead Burg's barrel thrower is no longer hit by his own barrel.
- **Water at your resolution**: a pool's reflection and ripples are drawn at the game's own ratio to your resolution, so reflections no longer crawl and dance when the camera moves at 4K.
- **The HUD scale holds through menus and shops**, and prompts, item pickups and messages are scaled with it.
- **Faster again**, with the same picture (4 to 6.5% more frames where the draw thread is the limit), and on the Steam Deck, Linux and phones the keyboard and mouse are read once a frame instead of four times.
- **DSfix 3.0 by DDTM is the recommended companion for quality-of-life features** (settings in the FPS Unlock's README); with DSfix 3.0 the sun and moon are no longer corrected twice, and with DSfix 2.4 as released in 2015 water no longer breaks into blocks.

## New

- **Protection against the 2022 remote code execution exploit.** The game copies a text from another player's network data into a fixed buffer without a limit, and a longer text overwrote what follows it on the stack, the return address included: the overflow behind the 2022 exploit. The copy now stops at the end of the buffer. It is installed at start-up whether the co-op is on or off. DSCM placed a guard at the same spot in 2022; checked by emulating the game's loop, its limit lies past the return address, so this one uses the buffer's own end.
- **Invasion searches say whose world was full and why.** A host publishes its numbers in its lobby, and the invader's log names every world the search found: whose it is, its version, its players (co-op players and invaders) against its `MaxPlayers` and `ExtraInvaders`, and what kept it from taking them: full, at its invader limit, invaded recently, a boss fight, invaders off, or another version. A co-op search logs the worlds it found too. Worlds of hosts older than 2.0.0 are named without their numbers.

## Install

Copy the files next to `DARKSOULS.exe`: `xinput1_3.dll`, `PTDE-Coop-FPS-Unlock.ini` and `dxvk_d3d9.dll` (DXVK is on by default). Upgrading from 1.9.0: replace `xinput1_3.dll` and `dxvk_d3d9.dll`, and keep your INI (it works as it is, and new settings take their defaults when it does not list them) or take the new one for its notes, after noting your `Password`. Everyone sets the same `Password` to play together; invaders find every Prepare to Co-op world whatever its password. Back up your saves; co-op characters are kept in their own save file. Steam Deck and Linux: launch option `WINEDLLOVERRIDES="xinput1_3=n,b" %command%`; on the Steam Deck keep the game's anti-aliasing off.
