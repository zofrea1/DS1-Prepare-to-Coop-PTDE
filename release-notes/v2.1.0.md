# Prepare to Co-op Edition v2.1.0

Every player can talk to and trade with NPCs, enemies revived by a rest stay alive, invaders keep their own Estus, and the DS1 FPS Unlock 2.5.0 inside. **Plays with 1.5.0 to 2.0.1** (the co-op protocol is the same). The fixes work in each player's own game (most in the guest's), so everyone should update.

**Includes the [DS1 PTDE FPS Unlock](https://github.com/zofrea1/DS1-FPS-Unlock-PTDE-Remastered) 2.5.0** (see its [release notes](https://github.com/zofrea1/DS1-FPS-Unlock-PTDE-Remastered/releases/tag/v2.5.0-PTDE-DX9)):

- **Effects keep their 30 FPS look at any frame rate.** Swaying particles no longer turn several times too often (the Blighttown spider offspring's magma tentacle keeps its flames on its body), effect trails keep their whole length above 60 FPS (they were cut to a quarter at 240), and smoke, sparks and embers come out as dense as in the original game at every frame rate (since 1.3.0 they were about a quarter too thin).
- **HUD and menus scaled by the game's own placement.** Each part of the HUD is scaled through the game's own transform for it, so overlapping parts stay in place; the letters go with their part (`UIScaleText`); and the start menu and its screens, the bonfire's screens, shops, conversations and message boxes (such as "X has died") can be scaled too, each toward its own anchor, with the mouse following (`UIScaleMenus`).
- **Prompts no longer hide a boss's health bar** (`FixBossBarOverlap`): the prompt and the item windows move up above it.
- **No black specks on grass with the game's anti-aliasing** (`FixAntialiasingDepth`), at no cost; the game as shipped has them too.
- **10-bit colour by default** (`HighPrecisionColor = true`): as smooth as 16-bit float at a quarter of its cost. `float` keeps 16-bit float.
- **Faster at high resolutions**: 263 to 308 FPS at Firelink in 4K with 4x anti-aliasing, the same picture.
- **RTX Remix**: the game's brightness option works under the RTX mod.
- **DSfix**: `YieldToDSFix` is only for using DSfix's own picture settings. To use DSfix 3.0 for its quality-of-life features beside the FPS Unlock's picture, leave it off (and `FixSun` on); the FPS Unlock's README gives the settings for both ways, which go in `PTDE-Coop-FPS-Unlock.ini`.

## Fixed

- **A player could not talk to or trade with an NPC after another player greeted it.** The talk scripts keep their own memory of a conversation in map flags beside the conversation flags, which are each player's own. Shared, the host's greeting of the Undead Merchant reached a guest who had not met him at home, and his script has no answer for that pair: the guest could not talk to him or trade. These 58 flags, used by no map event, now belong to each player, and a guest's talk scripts read them in the guest's own world. Checked against Prepare to Die Edition's own scripts.
- **Enemies revived by a rest died again at once and paid their souls twice.** After the host's rest, the host's report of the enemies' health from before the rest reached a guest's game 0.3 to 0.6 s after the world reset and set the enemies the guest had killed, back at full health, to 0 again; they died in both games and paid the guest and the host once more. A guest now keeps a living enemy's health against those reports for 2 s after a reset, and the host's list of the dead is held back in a load that a rest came during (a guest joining during the host's rest). Enemies that are meant to stay dead come back dead by their own map events in every game. Also, an enemy left standing at 0 HP with no death begun (moved by this game's own AI, its group active) gets the game's own death after 3 s, logged.
- **Invaders came home with more Estus than their bonfire gives.** An invader's flask is cut to 5 charges on arrival and the rest set aside, to come back once home. The game's own way home can refill the flask, and the set-aside charges then came on top of the refill: 10 before the invasion, 5 in it, 10 at home plus 5 = 15. They now come back only up to what the flask held on arrival; a refill above that is kept as it is.
- **A guest's own-world checks could get the host's answer.** When two of the game's threads read the guest's own world's flags at the same moment, one gave up and got the host's world's bit, so a treasure the guest had taken at home could come back. It now waits for the other.

## Install

Copy the files next to `DARKSOULS.exe`: `xinput1_3.dll`, `PTDE-Coop-FPS-Unlock.ini` and `dxvk_d3d9.dll`. Upgrading from 2.0.x: replace `xinput1_3.dll`; your INI keeps working, and new settings take their defaults when it does not list them (or take the new one for its notes, after noting your `Password`). Everyone sets the same `Password` to play together. Back up your saves; co-op characters are kept in their own save file. Steam Deck and Linux: launch option `WINEDLLOVERRIDES="xinput1_3=n,b" %command%`.

**Online play: no guarantees.** The mod modifies the game in memory. Use at your own risk; no responsibility is accepted for bans or lost progress (see the README and LICENSE).
