# Prepare to Co-op Edition v2.2.0

Smoother and faster with the DS1 FPS Unlock 2.6.0 inside, the Death Knell no longer brings back the enemies its player had killed, invaders are no longer stopped by the one-time fog walls, and a controller no longer goes dead on phones and Linux. **Plays with 1.5.0 to 2.1.0** (the co-op protocol is the same). The fixes work in each player's own game, so everyone should update.

**Includes the [DS1 PTDE FPS Unlock](https://github.com/zofrea1/DS1-FPS-Unlock-PTDE-Remastered) 2.6.0** (see its [release notes](https://github.com/zofrea1/DS1-FPS-Unlock-PTDE-Remastered/releases/tag/v2.6.0-PTDE-DX9)):

- **The game's mutexes without kernel calls** (`FastMutexes`, new): the game thread took them about 200 times a frame, each a call into Windows, and queued behind other threads at every hand-over. In a heavy Blighttown view the slow frames (one in twenty at 7.5 to 10 ms) are gone: the 95th percentile of the frame times went from 7.6 to 3.3 ms at 4K, and at 1280x720 the frame rate from 351 to 495 FPS.
- **Faster where the effects fill the view**: about 9% more frames in the Demon Ruins at 4K (filter states with anisotropic filtering answered as repeats, the effects' curves and helpers in registers, the bones' matrices with whole-row moves), the same picture.
- **The keyboard and mouse read once a frame on Windows too** (`CacheInputReads`), about 2% more frames where the game thread is the limit.
- **Better with DSfix 3.0**: the 10-bit colour now stays on beside DSfix, unless DSfix's own SMAA runs on the FPS Unlock's picture; and with `YieldToDSFix = true` the game draws its HUD and menus as it does without the FPS Unlock, so DSfix 3.0's HUD mod and its weapon durability bars find them.
- **Characters no longer snap between bright and dark** when you step closer (Quelaag's Domain's egg carriers most of all): `ShadowBias` is gone. It erased real shadows on what was near the camera and reached only one of the game's two shadow lookups. The 4096 shadow map keeps the fine weave away; an INI that still lists `ShadowBias` keeps working.
- **The controller no longer goes dead under Wine** (phones with GameNative or Winlator, Steam Deck, Linux): Wine could put its own desktop window in front of the game's, with nothing on screen to click it back, and the game ignored every button from then on. Under Wine that now counts as the game's own window.

## Fixed

- **The Death Knell brought back the enemies its player had killed.** A knell to a far teammate or summon sign goes through a load, which loads every map fresh: the enemies the knelling player had killed stood again in that player's game alone, while they stayed dead for everyone else, and their health flickered between the two games' values. The load now keeps the world's dead dead, as a death's reload does, and the wounded enemies keep their wounds.
- **Invaders were stopped by the one-time fog walls.** The white fog walls that go for good once walked through ("Traverse the white light", two in the Undead Burg, one or two in most areas) can only be walked through by the world's own player, and an invader's game kept them standing even where the host's party had walked through them long ago, so an invader could not pass. An invader's game now takes them all down, as it does the multiplayer walls.
- **A host who was down when a trip went kept the world's enemies dead.** A Homeward Bone, Darksign or Homeward taken while the host was down went through the host's respawn, which kept the dead enemies dead, while every guest's trip brought them back, as the game's own bone does. The host now starts the world over with everyone else.

## Install

Copy the files next to `DARKSOULS.exe`: `xinput1_3.dll`, `PTDE-Coop-FPS-Unlock.ini` and `dxvk_d3d9.dll`. Upgrading from 2.1.0: replace `xinput1_3.dll`; your INI keeps working, and new settings take their defaults when it does not list them. Everyone sets the same `Password` to play together. Back up your saves; co-op characters are kept in their own save file. Steam Deck and Linux: launch option `WINEDLLOVERRIDES="xinput1_3=n,b" %command%`.

**Online play: no guarantees.** The mod modifies the game in memory. Use at your own risk; no responsibility is accepted for bans or lost progress (see the README and LICENSE).
