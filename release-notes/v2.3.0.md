# Prepare to Co-op Edition v2.3.0 (DRAFT)

Remastered's swaying foliage, with the DS1 FPS Unlock 2.7.0 inside. **Plays with 1.5.0 to 2.2.0** (the co-op protocol is the same).

**Includes the [DS1 PTDE FPS Unlock](https://github.com/zofrea1/DS1-FPS-Unlock-PTDE-Remastered) 2.7.0:**

- **Foliage sways in the wind, as in Remastered** (`FoliageSway`, new, on): the grass, flowers, bushes, ferns, ivy and leaves Remastered made sway (Firelink, the Undead Burg, Darkroot Garden, Oolacile, Blighttown and more) now sway here too, with Remastered's wind motion, strength and speed; their shadows and reflections with them. `FoliageSwayStrength` and `FoliageSwaySpeed` in percent of Remastered's. About 1% at 4K where foliage fills the view. Each player's own picture: nothing is sent online.
- **Shadows further ahead missing beside DSfix 3.0** (fixed): with `ShadowMapResolution` at 4096 (the default) or 8192 and DSfix 3.0 installed, shadows ahead of the player appeared only up close, at a hard line. DSfix 3.0 did not pass on the clipping rectangles of three of the shadow map's four slices; they are now put back beneath it. With `YieldToDSFix = true`, `ShadowMapResolution` leaves the game's own 2048 map (DSfix decides the picture).
- **Ladder slides as fast as in the original game** (`FixLadderSlide`, new, on): at a high frame rate a slide down a ladder went up to 29% faster than at 30 FPS (each of its short animations cost a frame). It now takes as long as at 30 FPS, smoothly.
- **Control during Reverse Hollowing** (fixed): the bonfire softlock fix (`BonfireUnstick`) could stand the character up in the middle of Reverse Hollowing when its prompt was answered just as the character sat down. After the Reverse Hollowing or Kindle prompt it now waits until 8 seconds after it, longer than either takes; after any other menu it still steps in after a second.
- **Flickering surfaces with `DrawMapPieces = adjacent` or `all`** (fixed): in nine maps some surfaces exist twice, a detailed piece and a cheaper stand-in for the rooms that see it from afar (the Undead Burg and Parish, Anor Londo, Oolacile, Sen's Fortress and more). The game never draws both, but `adjacent` and `all` did, so they flickered over each other. Now the piece your room does not show is hidden while its twin is drawn.

## Install

As 2.2.0: replace `xinput1_3.dll`; your INI keeps working, and new settings take their defaults when it does not list them.
