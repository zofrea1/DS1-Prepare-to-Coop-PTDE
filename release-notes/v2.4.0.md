# Prepare to Co-op Edition v2.4.0

Watch your teammates fight across the map, see them wherever they go, and their slowest walk as smooth as yours; 4 GB of address space for the game; with the DS1 FPS Unlock 2.8.0 inside. **Plays with 1.5.0 to 2.3.0** (the co-op protocol is the same; a player on an older version just does not get this version's improvements on their own screen).

## Co-op

- **Enemies near your teammates, however far** (`EnemiesNearOtherPlayers`, new, on). The enemies around another player in your map are moved and drawn on your screen however far away they are: from the Undead Burg's bridge you watch a teammate fight the hollows in the streets below, their attacks, their deaths and their fall. Their game runs them, as it does for an enemy near both of you; yours only shows them: it never takes one over (so its AI never runs far from you), and their kills pay you nothing, as before. Regular enemies only: NPCs and bosses keep the game's rooms. Each one shown is updated every frame, so the more there are, the more it costs.
- **Other players across map boundaries.** A player who crossed from one map into the next (the Undead Burg onto the aqueduct to Firelink) vanished from your screen while their health bar went on, as your game only drew their character in the rooms it showed you of that map. Other players' characters (co-op players and invaders) are now drawn wherever their map is loaded.
- **Other players' slowest walk, smooth.** On your screen a player who walked slowly walked in bursts with pauses: their character was given the full walk's pace, caught up with their last position and stopped, and the game holds a stop for at least 0.2 seconds; at a high frame rate (many frames between two of their updates) that happened all the time. Each player now also sends how far their stick is tilted, so their character walks at their own pace, and a frame where it has caught up no longer stops it while its player is still walking. For co-op players and invaders alike; a player on an older version sends no tilt and their character walks as before.

## The game gets 4 GB of address space

- **`LargeAddressAware`** (new, on): `DARKSOULS.exe` gets the "large address aware" flag on the first start (the original kept as `DARKSOULS.exe.before-laa`), and from the next start the game has 4 GB of address space instead of 2. A host with 4K, DXVK and more of the world drawn had about 1.5 GB in use already and crashed at 1.7 GB with a teammate in the world; with up to eight players in a session, 2 GB can run out without any of that. On the Steam Deck and Linux, Proton gives the game 4 GB already, and nothing is changed. The log's heartbeat shows the address space left.

**Includes the [DS1 PTDE FPS Unlock](https://github.com/zofrea1/DS1-FPS-Unlock-PTDE-Remastered) 2.8.0:**

- **More of the world drawn around you** (`DrawMapPieces`): `adjacent` by default now (was `game`; phones keep `game`), the pieces seen from the rooms next to yours, so grass, ruins and torches no longer appear only once you reach a certain spot; `adjacent+` (new) for the rooms up to three steps away, with the detailed lower Burg from the Undead Burg's bridge (on the Steam Deck about 120 FPS on average there). An INI from 2.3.0 keeps its `DrawMapPieces = game`: set `adjacent` (or `adjacent+`) to have it. The detailed version of a surface now wins over the cheaper stand-in a room sees from afar (the lower Burg from the bridge), with no flicker where two versions meet; coarse far backdrops only where the game draws them; the map effects and lights of the added rooms, and (`DrawMapObjects`, new, on) their objects: ladders, doors, crates, barrels, item corpses. Characters keep the game's rooms (2.3.0 woke them too: Solaire could go missing from the Undead Burg with `all`). Each player's own picture: nothing is sent online.
- **Occlusion culling** (`OcclusionCulling`, new, on): the pieces, effects and objects `DrawMapPieces` adds are skipped while something hides them (at the Demon Ruins with `all`, Lost Izalith behind the walls: render thread 3.87 to 3.35 ms a frame).
- **Faster**: GPU work at 4K +8.7% (Firelink) to +18.5% (Darkroot); the water's mirrored pass is skipped while nothing that reads it shows.
- **The game's questions and plates follow the UI scale**: "Are you sure you want to quit the game?" with `UIScaleMenus`, "Used Dungeon Cell Key" with `UIScale`.
- **`YieldToDSFix`** leaves `FixAspect`, `FixAspectUI` and the refresh rate to DSfix, and `FixWater` steps back beside DSfix 3.0.

The details of each are in the FPS Unlock's [2.8.0 release notes](https://github.com/zofrea1/DS1-FPS-Unlock-PTDE-Remastered/releases/tag/v2.8.0-PTDE-DX9).

## Install

As 2.3.0: replace `xinput1_3.dll`; your INI keeps working, and new settings take their defaults when it does not list them. The first start sets the large address aware flag in `DARKSOULS.exe`; the game has 4 GB from the second start.
