# Prepare to Co-op Edition v2.5.0

The Death Knell no longer takes you out of the fight your body is in, and its view shows that fight; a firebomb thrower no longer stays with a far player's game; with the DS1 FPS Unlock 2.9.0 inside. **Plays with 1.5.0 to 2.4.0** (the co-op protocol is the same; a player on an older version just does not get this version's improvements on their own game).

## The Death Knell

- **The enemies keep fighting your body.** While you watch through the bell, your character kneels where you rang it. Until now the enemies there lost track of you, as if you stood where you were watching: the view moved your game's idea of where you are to your teammate, so the enemies around your body were updated only every fifth frame or less, your game kept losing them to a far player's game, and there they stood idle or stopped attacking (the Undead Burg's firebomb throwers stopped throwing). Now your game keeps your body as the centre of its world while the view is open: the world around it keeps loading, the enemies within 30 m keep their full pace, and they pursue and attack you where you kneel, as without the view. A hit still closes the view, as before. Dormant and dead enemies are left as the game has them.
- **The view shows your body.** Turn the view back toward where you knelt and you see your character and the enemies attacking it; they were invisible there, though your teammate's screen showed them. The view still shows everything around the teammate you watch: their enemies, objects and fights.

## Co-op

- **A thrower no longer stays with a far player's game.** An enemy in an attack stays with the game running it until the attack is over (up to 20 seconds), so that it is not cut off midway for everyone. An enemy repeating one attack reads as one long attack, and a firebomb thrower a teammate ran past stayed with that teammate's game, throwing at nobody in reach, for its whole 20 seconds while you stood under it. Now a regular enemy attacking in place changes hands after 8 seconds once the player whose game runs it is over 15 m away and another standing player is at least 5 m nearer. Bosses, enemies whose attacks carry them more than 3 m, and players fighting up close keep the full hold. The throw in progress at that moment can be cut short, once. Needs 2.5.0 on the game that runs the enemy.
- **Fixed: enemies you only watched counted toward your enemy scaling.** A teammate's fight shown on your screen by `EnemiesNearOtherPlayers` (since 2.4.0), or watched through the Death Knell, counted those enemies as yours under `VicinityScaling`, so they were scaled for one player more.

**Includes the [DS1 PTDE FPS Unlock](https://github.com/zofrea1/DS1-FPS-Unlock-PTDE-Remastered) 2.9.0:**

- **No flicker from another room's objects** (`DrawMapObjects`): an object placed for the rooms that see it up close is left out while the coarser map piece under it is drawn, as the game does from your room (from the Undead Burg's watchtower, the spire towers below flickered). Every map checked from the maps' own data.
- **`ReversedDepth`** (new, experimental, off; DXVK only): the depth buffer in 32-bit float, reversed. Little gain far away so far.
- **Less work for the draw distance's rules**, and two maps' overlapping pieces kept apart as within one map.
- **The "address space low" warning** no longer appears on the Steam Deck and Linux, where the game has 4 GB already.

The details of each are in the FPS Unlock's [2.9.0 release notes](https://github.com/zofrea1/DS1-FPS-Unlock-PTDE-Remastered/releases/tag/v2.9.0-PTDE-DX9).

## Install

As 2.4.0: replace `xinput1_3.dll`; your INI keeps working, and new settings take their defaults when it does not list them.
