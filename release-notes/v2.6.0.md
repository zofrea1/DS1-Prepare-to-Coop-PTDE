# Prepare to Co-op Edition v2.6.0

The co-op's settings in the game (FPS Unlock Settings), a co-op item used from the inventory no longer fires again at every press of use, and with the DS1 FPS Unlock 3.0.0 inside: lips and ledges climbed as at 30 FPS, no more enemies carried up walls, a cleaner picture in dark places and fixes for the Steam Deck. **Plays with 1.5.0 to 2.5.0** (the co-op protocol is the same; a player on an older version just does not get this version's improvements on their own game).

## Co-op

- **The co-op's settings in the game**: the FPS Unlock's new settings page (F1, or Y in the PC settings screen) has a Co-op page: the password (typed on the keyboard, shown as dots), players in a session, invaders, the invasion cooldown, the health bars over players, Crimson Blossoms, Bloody Mary, enemies scaled by nearby players, network updates and shared drops. Each change is written to the INI, and used at once when no session runs, otherwise when the current one ends (the health bars at once).
- **Fixed: a co-op item used from the inventory kept being used.** Used from the inventory menu, the Cursed Pendant (or another co-op item, or the Homeward Bone in a session) stayed the item in use: after it, every press of the use button used it again instead of the item on the quick bar, so the pendant played again and its curse went on and off. The game ends an item's use when it takes the item; these items are not taken, so their use was never ended. It now is, the way the game ends its own, and the next press uses the quick bar's item as it should.

**Includes the [DS1 PTDE FPS Unlock](https://github.com/zofrea1/DS1-FPS-Unlock-PTDE-Remastered) 3.0.0:**

- **FPS Unlock Settings** (new): an in-game page for the frame rate cap, refresh rate, HUD size, picture and draw distance (F1, or Y in the PC settings screen); changes are saved to the INI.
- **Every refresh rate in the game's PC settings** (it listed only 59/60 Hz); the rate chosen there is used, and the screen shows the rate in use.
- **Lips a little above a step are climbed as at 30 FPS** (`FixStepUp`, new, on): a character pushing against something but hardly moving is moved as at 30 FPS. The Great Hollow's Chloranthy Ring hollow, which could not be left at 60 to 240 FPS, is left in about a third of a second at any frame rate, as at 30. Enemies get it too.
- **Enemies no longer carried up walls** (`FixStepDown`): one of the Lower Undead Burg's door-ambush thieves shot 12 to 16 m into the sky at 120 FPS, carried up the wall beside its door. It now lands and fights as at 30 FPS.
- **No more whitish dots in dark places** (`HighPrecisionColor`): the dither into the 8-bit picture is now a fine noise, different per colour and new every frame, instead of a fixed grid.
- **10 bits to the screen** (`HighPrecisionOutput`, new, `auto`) with DXVK on an HDR (or Auto Color Management) display, and on the Steam Deck OLED in HDR: no dither at all.
- **Fixed: too dark with supersampling in exclusive fullscreen** (`Supersampling`, or a `RenderResolution` above the screen).
- **Steam Deck and DXVK**: no more grey specks in grass and leaves with anti-aliasing (`FixAntialiasingDepth`), and `AlphaToCoverage` now works on AMD graphics under DXVK (about 2.5% of the frame time on the Deck).
- **`BorderlessFullscreen = auto`** (the new default): borderless while the game runs on DXVK; with the game's own Direct3D 9 the window is left to the game (exclusive fullscreen).

The details of each are in the FPS Unlock's [3.0.0 release notes](https://github.com/zofrea1/DS1-FPS-Unlock-PTDE-Remastered/releases/tag/v3.0.0-PTDE-DX9).

## Install

As 2.5.0: replace `xinput1_3.dll`; your INI keeps working, and new settings take their defaults when it does not list them.
