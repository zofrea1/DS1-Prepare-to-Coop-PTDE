# Prepare to Co-op Edition v2.7.0

What you saw happen in a session goes home with you, Dark Souls' Steam achievements unlock (also for what your team does together), vagrants finally come, invaders and co-op players share dropped items, your game links with more players, other players place their feet on stairs, and the DS1 FPS Unlock 3.1.0 is inside. **Plays with 1.5.0 to 2.6.0** (the co-op protocol is the same; a player on an older version just does not get this version's improvements on their own game).

## Progress

- **World progress witnessed in a session goes home.** Until now only a boss's defeat went home with a guest. Now everything that happens in the host's world while you are in the session, wherever you stand, is yours too: both Bells of Awakening, Sen's Fortress's gate, New Londo's drain and its lifts, shortcuts and opened doors, Anor Londo's darkness, the Firekeepers' fates, and each map's own world progress, with what it needs at home (Sen's gate brings both bells). The endgame stays your own (the Lordvessel, the Lord Souls and the Kiln). An invader still takes home only a boss's defeat.
- **NPCs**: every NPC's death goes home, with that NPC's other states cleared. An NPC's step that another player's game made (a conversation, a move) goes home when that NPC stands on the same step in your world; one that does not line up stays in the host's world, as before.
- **Blighttown's bell heard nearby**: the game sends nothing for Quelaag's bell (only the Parish bell is heard by others). Players in Blighttown now hear it toll when it is rung.

## Steam achievements

- **Dark Souls' achievements unlock** (`SteamAchievements`, on). The game runs as Spacewar, whose achievements are not Dark Souls', so none unlocked. A small helper (Windows' rundll32 with this mod's DLL) now runs as Dark Souls beside the game and takes the game's unlocks; "all achievements" unlocks only when Steam says every other one is. It needs the Steam account that owns the game; without it nothing changes. While it runs Steam treats Dark Souls as running (it may show you playing it). Tested on Windows; on the Steam Deck it starts and reads your achievements.
- **What your team does together counts**: a bell rung or a boss with an achievement (Gravelord Nito, the Bed of Chaos, the Four Kings, Seath, Gwyndolin, Priscilla) defeated in another player's game in your co-op session unlocks it for you too, wherever you are in the world. An invader gets only what the game itself gives.

## Vagrants

Items a player lost and humanity they never recovered travel between players' games and come as vagrant enemies in the area they were lost in. With few players and short sessions they almost never did. Now:

- **They can come in the first world they reach** (`VagrantWanders`, 2; the game's 10), the stronger kind after twice that.
- **Each game keeps one 150 s** before passing it on (`VagrantStay`; the game's 300), and **passes on what it holds before Quit Game or closing** (`VagrantPassOnAtQuit`), where the game ended them.
- **One that reaches you while its area is loaded comes at once** (`VagrantCheckOnArrival`); the game waited for your next load there, and by then it had usually moved on.
- **A world rests 5 minutes after a vagrant comes** (`VagrantCooldown`, 300 s); the game's rest was 30 to 40 minutes and grew with each.
- **Humanity vagrants from any lost humanity** (`VagrantHumanity`, 1; the game needed a lost bloodstain with 5).
- **Firelink Shrine gets vagrant spots** (`VagrantSpotsFirelink`): the game's map has none there, so a vagrant lost in Firelink never came. It now comes at the nearest of three places (the bridge by the Ring of Sacrifice, Petrus's ruin, between the Firekeeper and Lautrec) with nobody standing on it.

## Invasions

- **Invaders and co-op players share dropped items**: an item an invader drops lies on the co-op players' floors too, and what the host and guests drop lies on the invader's.
- **An invader passes over the world it just invaded for 30 s**, while other worlds can take it.
- **Fixed: group invasions failed to reach slow hosts**: a host whose lobby had not yet seen the group's ask turned it away. It now lets it in when the asker arrives.
- **Worlds of an older version are told to update**: an invader whose search finds only worlds of another version knocks once on each, so their hosts see why they get no invaders. Long notices now show in pieces that fit the message box, and the search says how many worlds are on an older or newer version.

## Players and the network

- **Other players' feet on stairs and slopes** (`FootIkPlayers`, on): the other players' characters place their feet and lower their hips as yours does (NPCs' come with the FPS Unlock's `FootIkNpcs`).
- **More links with other players** (`LinkUnmoddedGames`, on). Your game only looked for Prepare to Co-op players; unmodded Prepare to Die Edition players on Spacewar could link with you, but not you with them. It now looks for them too (Prepare to Co-op players first), and each link brings what that game has gathered from its own: more messages, bloodstains, ghosts and vagrants. Co-op and invasions stay between Prepare to Co-op players.
- **The network in the log** (`NetworkEventLog`, on): bells, vagrants, miracle resonance and the other extras other players' games send, each as it arrives or summed up every 5 minutes, the games yours is linked with, and how many Prepare to Die Edition games are on Spacewar.
- **Up to 64 players** (`MaxPlayers`, 2 to 64, still 8 by default; experimental): more floating health bars, a bigger network heap and menu buffers, and the host's hand-out of enemies in linear time (64 players far apart: 50 ms a pass before, 0.2 ms now). Tested with NPC phantoms, not yet with that many people. Above eight players every game in the session needs 2.7.0 and a high enough `MaxPlayers`.

## Fixed

- **The Death Knell's view no longer takes over the enemies around the teammate it watches**: those enemies, updated in the kneeling game only to be shown, could be handed to it to run (when it stood within 64 m, or the teammate was in another area), and their kills paid it souls and the Evil Eye's heal.

**Includes the [DS1 PTDE FPS Unlock](https://github.com/zofrea1/DS1-FPS-Unlock-PTDE-Remastered) 3.1.0:**

- **Anti-aliasing switched by the mod** (`AntialiasingSamples`): `2`, `4` or `8` switch it on even when the game's own option is off (as it often is after DSfix), `game` leaves it to that option. With the default `2`, a game whose option is off now gets 2x anti-aliasing.
- **NPCs' feet on stairs and slopes** (`FootIkNpcs`, new, on).
- **Swaying trees** (`FoliageTreeSway`, new, off; contributed): the leaves of Firelink's, the Burg's, the Parish's, Sen's and Anor Londo's trees sway with the foliage.
- **The settings page from anywhere in the System menu** (Y), and on the Steam Deck, where it never showed.
- **Fixed: Darkroot Garden's bushes and flowers popping** with `DrawMapPieces = adjacent+`.

The details of each are in the FPS Unlock's [3.1.0 release notes](https://github.com/zofrea1/DS1-FPS-Unlock-PTDE-Remastered/releases/tag/v3.1.0-PTDE-DX9).

## Install

As 2.6.0: replace `xinput1_3.dll`; your INI keeps working, and new settings take their defaults when it does not list them. Every new setting is on by default except `FoliageTreeSway`.
