# Prepare to Co-op Edition v2.7.1

A patch release: Firelink Shrine's bonfire works again. **Plays with 1.5.0 to 2.7.0** (the co-op protocol is the same). The fix works in each player's own game, so everyone should update.

**Includes the [DS1 PTDE FPS Unlock](https://github.com/zofrea1/DS1-FPS-Unlock-PTDE-Remastered) 3.1.0**, unchanged (see its [release notes](https://github.com/zofrea1/DS1-FPS-Unlock-PTDE-Remastered/releases/tag/v3.1.0-PTDE-DX9)).

- **Fixed: Firelink Shrine's bonfire was out in 2.7.0.** No flame and no rest, for every player, in a session or not. 2.7.0 gave Firelink three vagrant spots (`VagrantSpotsFirelink`), and the one between the Firekeeper and Lautrec, above the New Londo lift, was on the ground around the shrine's bonfire. The game puts a bonfire out while an enemy stands on that ground, and the enemies waiting unseen at a vagrant spot count. That spot is removed. A vagrant lost in Firelink now comes on the bridge by the Ring of Sacrifice or by Petrus's ruin, whichever is nearer; neither is on a bonfire's ground, as none of the game's own vagrant spots are.
- **On 2.7.0 until you update:** `VagrantSpotsFirelink = false` in `PTDE-Coop-FPS-Unlock.ini` brings Firelink's bonfire back.

## Install

As 2.7.0: replace `xinput1_3.dll`; your INI keeps working.
